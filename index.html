I'm Heramb Salunkhe, a builder and product-minded engineer from Chiplun, India. Recently worked as an Intern shipping full-stack systems at Autonmis.

I've bounced through AI agents, link engines, data ops (testing different 3rd party integrations, brand-oriented visualizations), exploring different inference engines, and essentially data pipelines.
